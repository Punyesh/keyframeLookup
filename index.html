KEYFRAME LOOKUP

Look up animation staff by name and see their full credit history, grouped by role — right inside your browser. No install, no account, no server.
STEP 1
Drag this to your bookmarks bar

Not click — drag it up to your browser's bookmarks bar. If your bookmarks bar is hidden, show it first (Ctrl+Shift+B in Chrome).

📌 KeyFrame Lookup
↑ drag, don't click ↑
Using Firefox?

Firefox blocks long bookmarklets. Use this shorter one instead:

📌 KeyFrame Lookup (Firefox)
↑ drag, don't click ↑

If dragging fails, type javascript: by hand into the URL field, then paste the rest after it.

STEP 2
Go to the site

Open keyframe-staff-list.com and let it load normally.

STEP 3
Click your new bookmark

A small panel appears — drag it anywhere by its header. Type or paste names (one per line, or comma-separated), click Run lookup, then View Results once it's done for a full formatted page grouped by role.
